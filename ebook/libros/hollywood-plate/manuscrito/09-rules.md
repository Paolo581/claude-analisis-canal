# Part Two: Eat Like Them

## Chapter 9. The five rules of the Hollywood plate after 65

Eight stars, eight very different tables, and underneath them the same five rules. Here they are in one place. If you remember nothing else from this book, remember these.

### Rule 1. Protein at every meal, the size of your palm

Jane Fonda's rule, and the one that matters most after sixty-five. Each of your three meals should carry about 25 grams of protein: two eggs and yogurt at breakfast, fish or beans or cottage cheese at lunch, a palm-sized piece of chicken, fish or meat at dinner. Without it, the body quietly eats its own muscle, and muscle is what keeps you out of the hospital. A quick reference:

| Food | Protein |
|---|---|
| 1 egg | 6 g |
| 1 cup Greek yogurt | 20 g |
| 1 cup cottage cheese | 25 g |
| 3 ounces chicken, fish or lean meat | 22 to 26 g |
| 1 can tuna or salmon | 25 to 30 g |
| 1 cup cooked beans or lentils | 15 to 18 g |
| 1 ounce cheese | 7 g |
| 1 cup milk | 8 g |
| 2 tablespoons peanut butter | 8 g |
| 1 small handful of nuts | 5 to 6 g |

### Rule 2. Half the plate is vegetables

Audrey's rule, and Martha's. Look at your dinner plate. Half of it should be vegetables: salad, roasted tray, steamed greens, soup, a stir-fry. The other half is split between your protein and a small portion of starch: a cup of pasta, a scoop of rice, a potato, a slice of good bread. Frozen vegetables count. Canned, rinsed, count. A bag of spinach stirred into anything counts.

### Rule 3. The small plate, no seconds

Sophia's rule, and Julia's. Use a nine-inch plate, not a twelve. Serve once. Put the pot away before you sit down. Eat slowly, at the table, and stop when you are satisfied, not full. Twenty minutes is how long it takes your stomach to tell your brain. If you are still hungry twenty minutes after the meal, have a piece of fruit.

### Rule 4. A little of everything, not none of some things

Dolly's rule, and Betty's. Do not ban foods. Bans end in binges at eleven at night. Have the real cornbread, the real pudding, the hot dog, the slice of cake, in a small portion, at the table, on a day you choose, and enjoy it completely. Then the kitchen is closed.

### Rule 5. Nothing sweet to drink, and nothing after dinner

Clint's rule, and Betty's early bedtime. Water, tea, coffee, milk, and one glass of wine or one drink if your doctor allows. No soda, no sweetened coffee drinks, no juice as a beverage. And after dinner, one square of chocolate or a cup of herbal tea, and the kitchen is closed. The hours between seven and eleven at night are where the weight comes from.

### The plate, drawn

Picture a nine-inch plate divided like a clock. From twelve to six, vegetables. From six to nine, your protein. From nine to twelve, your starch. A glass of water beside it. A piece of fruit after. That is lunch and dinner for the rest of your life, and it is also exactly what Sophia Loren's grandmother served in 1940.

## Chapter 10. Losing weight after 65 without losing your strength

Here is a conversation many women have had with a doctor.

"You should lose some weight."

"How?"

"Eat less and move more."

And then nothing. No menu, no warning that the usual way of losing weight is actually dangerous after sixty-five, no mention that half of what you lose on a crash diet is muscle, not fat. So here is the chapter the doctor did not have time for.

### Why the old way fails after 65

When a younger woman cuts her food in half, she loses fat and some muscle. When a woman over sixty-five does it, she loses fat and a great deal of muscle, because her body is already losing muscle every year and the diet speeds it up. She ends the diet lighter but weaker: less able to climb stairs, more likely to fall, slower to recover from illness. Then the weight comes back as fat, and she is worse off than before. Doctors see it constantly.

The research is clear on what works instead, and it is almost exactly what the stars in Part One do.

### The gentle way, in five steps

**1. Decide whether you need to.** Being ten or fifteen pounds over the chart after sixty-five is not dangerous and may be protective. What matters is where the weight sits (around the middle is worse than on the hips), whether your blood sugar and blood pressure are good, and whether you can move. If your doctor says the numbers are fine and you can climb a flight of stairs, you may not need to lose anything. Ask.

**2. Keep protein high while eating less of everything else.** This is the whole trick. Cut the starch, the sugar, the snacks, the drinks, the portions. Do not cut the eggs, fish, chicken, beans, yogurt and cheese. Aim for your 25 grams at every meal, even on the days you eat less. Studies of older adults who lose weight this way keep almost all their muscle.

**3. Lift something.** Two or three times a week, the soup-can routine from our first book, or any strength class at the senior center. This tells your body to keep the muscle and burn the fat. Walking is wonderful for your heart, but it does not protect muscle the way lifting does. Both.

**4. Slow is the speed.** Half a pound to one pound a week. Not more. Faster than that is muscle going. At that speed, fifteen pounds takes four to six months. That sounds slow until you remember that every diet you did faster than that came back.

**5. Eat the Hollywood plate.** Protein at every meal, half the plate vegetables, small plate, no seconds, nothing sweet to drink, kitchen closed after dinner. The menu in the next chapter does this for you.

### Where the weight actually comes from

Most women are surprised when they write down everything they eat for three days. The meals are usually fine. The weight comes from:

- Sweet drinks, including juice, sweetened coffee, and "a little" soda
- The evening hours in front of the television
- Portions that have grown over years without anyone noticing
- "Healthy" packaged foods: granola bars, flavored yogurt, cereal, crackers
- Eating standing up, from the pot, while cooking
- Skipping breakfast and then eating too much at four in the afternoon

Fix those six things and most women lose weight without changing dinner at all.

### Three warnings

**Do not skip meals.** It leads to the four o'clock binge and it starves muscle.

**Do not go below about 1,200 calories a day without a doctor watching.** Older women who eat too little lose bone, lose muscle, get dizzy, fall, and get sick more often.

**If you are losing weight without trying, stop reading this chapter and call your doctor.** Unintentional weight loss after sixty-five is a warning sign, not good news.
