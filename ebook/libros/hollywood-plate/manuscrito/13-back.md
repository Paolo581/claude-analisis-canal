## Kitchen measures

- 1 cup = 8 fluid ounces = 16 tablespoons
- 1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoons
- "The size of your palm" = about 3 to 4 ounces of cooked meat or fish
- "A small handful" of nuts = about 1 ounce, 12 almonds or 7 walnut halves
- A 9-inch plate is the small plate. Measure yours.
- Oven: 400°F for fish and vegetables, 425°F for chicken and roasting

## A note for your doctor

Take this page to your next appointment.

*I am following a menu built on the Mediterranean pattern: about 1,400 to 1,600 calories a day, 75 to 90 grams of protein spread across three meals, half the plate vegetables, fish twice a week, beans twice a week, olive oil, little added sugar, and no more than one alcoholic drink a day. I am also doing light strength exercise two or three times a week. Is this appropriate for me, and is there anything I should change because of my medications or conditions, especially blood thinners, kidney function, diabetes, or blood pressure?*

Foods in this book that can interact with common medicines: leafy greens and warfarin (keep the amount steady from day to day rather than avoiding them); grapefruit and many heart and cholesterol medicines; alcohol and nearly everything. Your pharmacist can check your list in five minutes for free.
