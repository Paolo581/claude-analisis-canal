Why we wrote them

We spend our weeks on how the stars fell. These books are about how they stayed standing.

Every week on our channel we tell the stories the magazines never printed. The secret marriages, the fortunes that vanished, the last days. But after reading thousands of your comments, we kept seeing the same questions: how did the ones who lasted actually do it? What did they eat? How did Dick Van Dyke start over at 86? How did Betty White live forty years alone and never seem lonely?

So we went back through the memoirs, cookbooks, interviews and documentaries of the stars who stayed strong, slim and loved into their 80s, 90s and 100s, and wrote down what they did every day. Not the glamour. The routine. Then we checked each habit against what doctors and researchers say today, and turned it into something a woman over 65 can do this week, in her own kitchen, with her own phone.

Three books. Body, table and heart. None of it needs a gym, a diet or a dating service.
