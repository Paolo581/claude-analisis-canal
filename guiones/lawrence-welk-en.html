Lawrence Welk was born on March eleventh, nineteen oh three, in a farmhouse near Strasburg, in Emmons County, North Dakota. The walls were wood on the outside and packed sod on the inside, which is how people built on that prairie when there were no trees and no money.

And they had come to America in the early eighteen nineties from the Kutschurgan district near Odessa, in what was then the Russian Empire and is Ukraine today.
