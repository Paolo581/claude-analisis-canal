October, nineteen fifty-five. Rebel Without a Cause opened in American theaters, and its director, Nicholas Ray, became the man who turned James Dean into a legend and Natalie Wood into a star.

A black eye patch, three young stars who never reached old age, and a manuscript that sat in a drawer for almost seventy years. To understand how those pieces fit, you have to go back to a small town on the banks of the Mississippi River.
