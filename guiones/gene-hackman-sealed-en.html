Hantavirus. It's carried by deer mice, and people catch it by breathing in dust from the droppings, urine or nests of infected rodents. It's rare. New Mexico sees a handful of cases a year. It starts like the flu. Fever, aches, exhaustion, sometimes for a week or more. Then, often very suddenly, the lungs fill with fluid.
